# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

The TodoDoneYet project is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and constructive environment for everyone who contributes to or uses the project.

## Expected Behavior

Contributors are expected to:

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Communicate constructively.
- Welcome different perspectives and ideas.
- Focus discussions on improving the project.
- Give useful and respectful feedback.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment or discrimination.
- Personal attacks or insults.
- Threats or intimidation.
- Deliberate disruption of project discussions.
- Publishing private or sensitive information without permission.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it privately to the project maintainers through the repository's available contact or reporting channels.

All reports should be handled respectfully and as confidentially as reasonably possible.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may take appropriate action when this Code of Conduct is violated, including removing inappropriate content or restricting participation in project activities.
